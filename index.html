Behind
the scenes.

How we keep characters consistent: before a single scene is generated, everyone in the film gets a character sheet with four views. Face close-up, full body front, side profile and back.

That sheet is the reference for every shot, which is why Alvaro has the same face at the door and at the table, and why Caramelo keeps his torn ear.

  • Story first A script with a turn, written before anything is rendered.
  • Consistent characters One four-view sheet per character, human or cat.
  • Product close-ups Shots that linger on the product and the brand details.
  • Sound design Music and sound built for the cut, not pasted under it.
  • Logo card Every film closes on your brand.

Why it works.

  • Feeling is what people keep.

    A feature list asks for attention. A story earns it, and the brand is there when the feeling lands.

  • The characters hold together.

    Same face, same coat, same cat in every shot. Nothing breaks the spell halfway through.

  • The product gets its moment.

    It enters when the story needs it, in close-up, so it is wanted rather than announced.

  • Made by someone who runs ads.

    The films are written by a Meta Ads specialist, with the placement, the format and the test in mind.
